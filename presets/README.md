# Installing a preset

Choose one folder:

- Vibrant - Brighter colors
- Blue & Gold - Protanomaly and deuteranomaly
- Blue & Gold - Stronger separation, protanopia and deuteranopia
- Coral & Cyan - Tritanomaly
- Coral & Cyan - Stronger separation, tritanopia
- Grayscale - Experimental, monochromacy

These are starting points to try, not validated accessibility guarantees. Grayscale depends on brightness alone and its darker markers may be difficult to see against dark backgrounds.

Close Tokyo Xtreme Racer, then copy that folder's `.pak`, `.ucas`, and `.utoc` files into:

```text
TokyoXtremeRacer/TokyoXtremeRacer/Content/Paks/~mods
```

Create `~mods` if it does not exist. Remove older `RivalColors*` mod files first. Keep all three files together and install only one preset at a time: every preset uses the same filenames.

The configurator provides these same palettes and lets you adjust individual colors.
